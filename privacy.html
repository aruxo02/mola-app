Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

1. Data controller

Moises Lopez Otero Studio Programistyczne, Osiedle Willowe 5 lok. 1, 31-901 Kraków, Poland, NIP (tax ID) 6793218612. Contact: studiomlopez@gmail.com. This policy explains how we process personal data when you use the MOLA mobile app, in accordance with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

2. Data we collect

  • Account data: name, email address, profile photo and a unique user ID provided when you sign in with Google or Apple (with Apple you may hide your email).
  • Profile and content: username, bio, and content you create or publish (recipes, photos, menus, comments, ratings and YouTube links), people you follow, followers, and reports you submit.
  • App usage and preferences: settings, language, progress, favourites and in-app activity.
  • Device and technical data: device model, OS version, app version, IP address, crash logs, diagnostic data, push notification token.
  • Advertising data: advertising identifier (IDFA/AAID) and ad interaction data, only as permitted by your consent choices.

We do not collect precise location, contacts or payment data.

3. Purposes and legal bases

  • Providing the app, your account and its features, including publishing and social features – performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • Security, moderation of reported content, preventing abuse, crash diagnostics and service improvement – legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • Usage analytics, push notifications and personalised advertising – consent (Art. 6(1)(a)), which you can withdraw at any time in the app's privacy settings or your device settings. Without consent, non-personalised ads may be shown.
  • Complying with legal obligations – Art. 6(1)(c).

4. Processors and third parties

  • Google Firebase (Google Ireland Ltd / Google LLC): Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Storage, Cloud Messaging, Crashlytics, Analytics, Remote Config. Firebase privacy
  • Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform for consent. How Google uses data
  • Apple (Sign in with Apple, App Store, push notifications) and Google (Google Sign-In, Google Play).
  • YouTube – only when you open a linked video; YouTube's own policy applies.

On iOS we ask for permission via App Tracking Transparency before using the advertising identifier for tracking.

5. International transfers

Some providers may process data outside the European Economic Area, including in the USA. Such transfers rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and/or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

6. Public content

Content you publish and your public profile (username, photo, bio) are visible to other users. Do not include personal data you do not want to share.

7. Retention

We keep account data and content while your account exists. When you delete your account, your personal data and content are deleted within 30 days, except backups (purged within 90 days) and data we must keep by law or to handle open abuse reports. Analytics and crash data are retained per Google's settings (up to 14 months for analytics, 90 days for crash reports).

8. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict and object to processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time. To exercise them write to studiomlopez@gmail.com. You may also lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority, in Poland the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO, uodo.gov.pl), or in your country of residence.

9. Children

MOLA is not directed to children under 13, and in the EU users under 16 (or the lower age set by their country) may use it only with parental consent. We do not knowingly collect data from children; if you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections and stored with access controls provided by Google Cloud.

11. Changes

We may update this policy; material changes will be announced in the app. The date at the top shows the latest version.
