Account & Data Deletion

Last updated: 16 October 2026

You can delete your MOLA account and all associated data at any time.

Option 1 – In the app

  1. Open MOLA and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile → Settings → Delete account.
  3. Confirm. You may be asked to sign in again with Google or Apple.

Option 2 – By email

If you no longer have the app, email studiomlopez@gmail.com with the subject “MOLA: Delete my account” from the email linked to your account (or include your username). We will verify the request and confirm deletion within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account, profile (name, email, photo, username, bio) and sign-in link.
  • All content you created or published, ratings, follows and progress.
  • Your push notification tokens and preferences.

What may be kept

  • Encrypted backups, purged within 90 days.
  • Anonymous aggregated statistics, and analytics/crash data per Google retention (up to 14 months).
  • Data we must retain by law or to handle abuse reports.

Signed in with Apple? You can also revoke access in iOS Settings → Apple ID → Sign-In & Security → Sign in with Apple.
